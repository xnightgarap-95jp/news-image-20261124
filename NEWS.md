## LEAD 1 — STRAIT OF HORMUZ · MIDDLE EAST
**A fourth tanker hits a mine as Iran shuts the Strait further**
A fourth tanker struck a mine in the Strait of Hormuz, Iran's
Revolutionary Guards said on Tuesday, as Tehran ordered the waterway
closed "further day by day". Qatar pressed both sides to return to
talks, while Iran's parliament speaker and senior negotiator Mohammad
Bagher Ghalibaf said there would be no negotiations until Washington
met seven conditions Tehran has set out. The standoff has moved from
tanker strikes toward a contest over maritime closure.
- Middle East Eye: fourth tanker hits mine in the Strait of Hormuz.
- Euronews: Iran orders Hormuz closed "further day by day" as Qatar
  seeks to revive peace talks.
- Kayhan (Tehran), 24 Nov 21:42 +03:30: IRGC Navy reported the
  explosion of a fourth oil tanker after it struck a naval mine east
  of the strait "at dawn Tuesday"; Army chief Hatami said Tuesday the
  strait stays closed until the seven conditions are met.
- Cumhuriyet Daily, 24 Nov 16:38: Iran said Tuesday a fourth oil
  tanker exploded and caught fire after striking a naval mine in
  restricted waters east of the Strait of Hormuz.
- PressTV, 24 Nov 13:40 GMT: a fourth oil tanker exploded and caught
  fire after striking a naval mine in the eastern Strait of Hormuz.

## 6 — SEOUL · ASIA-PACIFIC
**Russian jets enter Korea's defence zone**
South Korea's Joint Chiefs of Staff said several Russian aircraft flew
inside South Korea's air defence identification zone over waters
between the Korean Peninsula and Japan. South Korea scrambled jets;
an evacuation warning was briefly issued and then corrected — the
aircraft entered the identification zone, not sovereign airspace.
- Just Security, Early Edition 24 Nov: several Russian aircraft flew
  inside South Korea's air defense identification zone.
- Chanakya Lens, 24 Nov: South Korea scrambles jets as Russian
  aircraft enter defense zone.

## 7 — BERLIN · EUROPE

The ministers also reached a preliminary agreement on additional
interceptor missiles for Ukraine.
- The Kyiv Independent (via The Global Eye, 24 Nov): Germany first

- La Mediterranee, 24 Nov: Germany and Ukraine reach an initial deal

## 8 — BAGHDAD · MIDDLE EAST
**Iraq orders ministry to run Kurdish oilfields**
Iraq's prime minister ordered the federal oil ministry to manage
oilfields in areas under Kurdish security control, the state news
agency said on Tuesday, potentially deepening tensions between Baghdad
and the semi-autonomous Kurdistan region. The order covers ministry
fields in Diyala, Kirkuk, Salahuddin and Nineveh provinces and follows
an oil smuggling investigation at the North Oil Company.
- Reuters / Al Arabiya (via The Global Eye, 24 Nov): Iraqi PM orders
  ministry to manage oilfields in areas under Kurdish security control.
